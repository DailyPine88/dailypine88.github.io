Privacy policy

Prize Draw Hunter is used only by its owner, with the owner's own Google account.

What it accesses

With permission, the Gmail account the owner connects: to create and send competition entry emails, to recognise entry confirmations, winner notifications and claim deadlines, to label those emails, and to send notifications to the owner. It never deletes email.

Where the data goes

Data is stored on the owner's private server. It is not sold, not shared for marketing and not used for advertising. Short excerpts of competition-related emails may be sent to Anthropic's Claude API solely to classify them (for example confirmation or winner). Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
